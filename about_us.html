About Us

Makan Watches is a premium watch brand providing luxury, sports, smart and casual watches at affordable prices.

Our mission is to deliver high-quality watches with modern design and advanced technology.

Welcome to Makan, where luxury meets innovation in the heart of India's craftsmanship. Since our inception in 2015, we've been rewriting the narrative of luxury products by making elegance accessible to all. At Makan, our vision is clear to lead the Luxury Fashion industry with a reputation for delivering unparalleled value and unwavering excellence. We aspire to foster a deep sense of patriotism and pride among every Indian by offering high-quality, made-in-India luxury products. Our mission is simple yet profound: to ensure that every individual, regardless of their background, can own a Makan product - a symbol of luxury at exceptional value for money. With Makan, owning a piece of luxury isn't just a dream; it's a celebration of Indian craftsmanship and a testament to one's discerning taste. Join us on this journey, where every timepiece tells a story of heritage, elegance, and the indomitable spirit of India.
